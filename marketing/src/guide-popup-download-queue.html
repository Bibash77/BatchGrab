Practical workflow guide

Can you close the popup during a batch?

First distinguish an accepted job from an unsent selection.

A popup closing should not be confused with a batch being submitted. Selecting files is not the same as saving download jobs.

A small safe trial

  1. Review a few files and their final names.
  2. Click Download Selected and wait for the jobs-saved confirmation.
  3. Close the popup while keeping Chrome running.
  4. Reopen it and inspect completed, pending and failed outcomes.
  5. Check the actual downloaded files, not just the interface.

Batch Grab stores accepted jobs in its background queue. Popup closure and a service-worker restart were tested on controlled fixtures, including a 120-file batch. That is an engineering test, not a guarantee for every device, website or shutdown scenario.

Know the boundaries

Keep Chrome running. Browser shutdown and operating-system suspension remain uncertified. Recent unsubmitted edits use a short draft-save delay; do not assume a selection was submitted because you closed the popup. If Chrome asks where to save every file, review its download settings before a large batch.

Check errors on reopening. A failed attempt that is retried remains distinguishable from an unresolved failure. Retry only after investigating the cause; an access restriction requires permission from the source, not repeated attempts to bypass it.
