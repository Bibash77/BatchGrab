Keep the source with the file

“Completed” is a transfer status.
You still check the document.

The source index connects the original page and URL with actual saved paths and reported failures. It stays on your computer until you choose to share it.

  • Accepted jobs continue after the popup closes; keep Chrome running.
  • See interrupted downloads and retry failed attempts manually.
  • Keep source URLs, requested names, actual paths and outcomes in one local CSV.
  • Check the contents of important files. A saved response can still be the wrong document.
