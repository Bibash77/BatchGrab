# Batch Grab — first customer marketing launch

Prepared 2026-10-22, Asia/Tokyo. Status: beta 0.3.1, zero confirmed customers, $0 actual revenue. No public deployment, posts, messages, account changes, ads or payments were performed. The goal is one task-qualified beta user, a completed real trial, then a genuine retained $5 software purchase.

## Product and promise

**A folder you can hand over.** Collect accessible web documents, review their names and section folders, and retain a local source/outcome index. Target independent researchers, consultants, freelancers and virtual assistants who prepare 20–100-file public document packs and can choose their own tools.

The current extension scans one page, prioritizes content/documents, proposes editable names from visible link/nearby labels and section headings, saves via Chrome, tracks actual outcomes, supports manual retry, and exports a local CSV. Image workflows and advanced options remain available. It does not read inside PDFs, combine multiple pages, import spreadsheets, ZIP/convert files or bypass access restrictions.

Evidence inspected: the source manifest and six-file package match the verified 0.3.1 hashes. October 19 browser reports cover five controlled cases, three public pages, correct actual filenames/bytes, failures/retry, Japanese outputs and a 120-file worker restart. The 42 helper assertions were rerun during launch preparation. Public examples (IRS, NASA, Books to Scrape) are tests, not supported-site guarantees or customers. No runtime feature was added for marketing.

## Deliverable and offer

- Local static website: landing, real-screenshot walkthrough, beta download/install instructions, proposed pricing/interest page, FAQ/privacy draft and five educational guides.
- Real screenshot assets are copied byte-for-byte from evidence. Illustrative filenames and PDFs are labelled fictional fixtures. No testimonial, customer counter or guaranteed savings.
- $5 once is a **proposal for the delivered Document Handoff version**, not a live checkout. Free evaluation exposes the entire workflow without an enforced gate. Do not mislabel a donation, friend purchase, unpaid invoice or conditional promise as a software sale.
- Form text is prepared locally; the user can copy/download it or open an email draft to **ronaldoibibash310@gmail.com**, then manually choose to send. Nothing is silently submitted or recorded centrally. No marketing email consent is inferred.

## Seven-day execution after approval

**Day 0:** owner confirms public hostname/hosting, publisher contact/retention wording, distribution/license and exact drafts. Deploy the static archive only after explicit approval. Check public beta ZIP, fixture, links and privacy/contact page. Keep checkout inactive. Record live URL; no invented domain placeholder is published.

**Day 1:** post C01 once in its designated thread, using the product-offer draft without surveys/interview requests. Check the actual comment is visible logged out; if removed, stop and inspect/moderator clarification—never evade filters or repeatedly repost. Log actual permalink/date.

**Day 2:** after separately approving C02, post one disclosed maker announcement with a real screenshot and beta link. No automated DMs, voting requests or cross-post burst. Answer replies honestly and only follow up with opt-ins.

**Days 2–5:** aim to conduct up to three qualified trials, a target rather than a forecast. Qualify a current allowed page, 20–100 files, naming/source-handoff problem, next batch and authority to install/spend. Ask for no client documents or private URLs. A generic installation tester is not a qualified buyer.

**Trial:** agree the expected file set independently. Try the best appropriate free workflow (including SMD’s Link text mask when relevant), then Batch Grab on the same subset; alternate order across people. Measure setup, corrections, completeness and cleanup separately. Verify saved contents and source-index use. Fix correctness free. Record permission before retaining quotes/screenshots.

**Days 5–7:** after success, ask whether they want the delivered version for $5 once over the adequate free alternatives. If yes, confirm scope and request separate owner authorization for an existing legitimate payment/invoice route. No payment setup has been performed. A cleared payment + delivery + retained purchase is the first sale; an interest email is not.

## Measurement and stop rules

FIRST_SALE_TRACKER.csv is intentionally header-only. Enter actual public permalinks and periods, beta emails received, qualified people, completed tasks, actual offers and cleared/refunded amounts with evidence. Blank means unknown/unmeasured, not zero. QA visits, copied drafts, downloads alone and optional interest choices are not beta requests, conversion, customers or revenue.

No analytics is installed. Landing visits remain blank unless the approved host supplies a clearly defined aggregate measurement. HTTP page requests are not automatically unique visitors; record the method and filter owner/QA traffic. An optional utm_source travels into the voluntary draft without cookies, storage or a tracking request.

If the two placements produce no qualified trial within seven days/three founder recruiting hours, inspect installation/channel fit and use one genuine warm introduction before more engineering. This is a channel result, not proof of no demand. If three task-qualified trials show the free tool is sufficient and nobody buys, reject this paid hypothesis. Do not add features to rescue a vague pitch.

A retained $5 gross sale exceeds $1 revenue for that calendar month. One-time licenses are not MRR. A new genuine sale in each subsequent month is required to meet a monthly actual-revenue goal; averaging one sale over several months is different. Ask a satisfied purchaser for one voluntary referral/case-study permission, then repeat the same task-qualified route. No forecast or fake conversion rate is used. Track fees, refunds and support; avoid paid acquisition and site-specific promises.
