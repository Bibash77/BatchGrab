Privacy and permissions

Extension processing

When you invoke Batch Grab, it reads exposed resource URLs, labels, headings, image context and dimensions on that page to display files and suggest names. Drafts, selected URLs, requested names, source pages, local saved paths, Chrome download IDs, statuses, errors and timestamps are stored in your local Chrome extension storage. They are used only for scanning, naming and download outcomes. No account or extension telemetry is enabled. Batch Grab does not sell or upload this data.

Requests and exports

Previews and downloads contact source hosts. Their normal network logs, sessions and access rules apply. Exporting CSV creates a local file containing source URLs and local paths; it can contain private tokens. Review before sharing.

Retention and deletion

The latest draft remains until replaced, cleared with Options → Clear saved scan, or uninstall. Settled attempts are capped at 500 and 30 days, pruned on queue activity or worker startup. Pending jobs are retained. Use Clear finished history for settled records. Removing the extension clears its local storage but does not remove downloaded files, exported CSV or Chrome’s own download records.

Website and support

This website enables no analytics service, tracking cookies or remote forms. When hosted on Vercel, requests for these pages, screenshots and downloads pass through Vercel. Vercel may process IP addresses, request details and operational/security logs to deliver and protect the website. Hosting records are subject to Vercel’s policies; Batch Grab does not promise a fixed retention period for those records. See Vercel’s privacy notice. The extension does not send your scanned webpages, source documents, filenames or local download history to this website. Voluntary support email is processed by Google’s Gmail service and the publisher. Send only non-sensitive descriptions. Contact ronaldobibash310@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to necessary records.

Permissions and policy

activeTab/scripting: scan the invoked page. downloads: save and inspect transfers. storage: local jobs and drafts. alarms: reconcile jobs. No persistent host access. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. No browsing URLs, source documents or filenames are sent to analytics.
