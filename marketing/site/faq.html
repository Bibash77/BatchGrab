Answers before your first download

Is it free?

Yes. This release has no account, checkout, subscription or paid license.

What permissions are requested?

activeTab and scripting scan only the page you invoke it on. downloads starts and checks transfers. storage saves local drafts and jobs. alarms reconciles the background queue. No persistent all-sites host permission is requested.

Does it contact any servers?

There is no Batch Grab backend or extension telemetry. Image previews and downloads contact their source websites, which receive ordinary network requests. Chrome controls their session and download behavior.

What is retained?

The latest page draft is kept locally until replaced, cleared in Options, or uninstall. Finished download history is limited to 500 attempts and 30 days, pruned on queue activity or worker startup. Pending jobs remain until resolved. Clear finished history removes settled records; exported and downloaded files are separate.

What are the limits?

500 files per batch, 1,000 pending jobs, 10,000 scan results and 60 rendered cards per page. A scan draft over 2 MB is not cached. Queue metadata is bounded at 4 MB; use smaller batches if needed.

Which websites work?

Normal HTTP(S) pages exposing image or document resources. Responsive candidates, common lazy attributes, open shadow DOM and accessible same-origin frames are scanned. Optional CSS/loaded resources are uncertain candidates. Closed shadow DOM, cross-origin frames, automatic scrolling and protected content are not supported.

Can I close the popup?

After jobs are accepted, yes; keep Chrome running. Popup closure and worker restart are tested. Full browser shutdown and OS suspension are not certified.

Does it understand documents?

No. Suggestions use page labels and heuristics. It does not read PDF contents or guarantee file correctness. Review names and uncertain image associations.

How do I change language?

Choose English or 日本語 in the extension header. Only interface text is translated; source labels and filenames stay unchanged.

How do I report a failure?

Email a redacted error and task summary. Do not send tokens, private URLs or documents. Check Chrome Downloads for the original error.
