Why two downloaders show different file counts

Compare resource URLs and relevance before comparing totals.

Two extensions can scan the same apparent webpage and show different totals without either having found more useful documents.

Four things to check

  1. Page state: images and links may appear after loading, interaction or scrolling.
  2. Filters: one list may include ordinary webpage links, logos and icons, while another shows only document candidates.
  3. Responsive images: a small thumbnail and a larger srcset alternative can be two URLs for the same picture.
  4. Duplicates: tools may deduplicate exact URLs or group size variants differently.

Compare the intended files

Use the same page, login state, wait and scroll position. Compare actual unique URLs, then inspect which ones are relevant and accessible. A link named “Download” is not proof that it serves a downloadable file.

Batch Grab keeps ordinary files and links accessible in All files, while Suggested prioritizes likely content and documents. Additional uncertain image alternatives appear in Review. Those classifications are rules, not semantic understanding or a promise that every candidate will download. No tool should be expected to recover protected content simply because its displayed count is larger.
